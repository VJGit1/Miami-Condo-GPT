Shore Club — Offering Plan Excerpt (Fixture)

Amenities: The Shore Club residence includes beach access, pool deck, fitness center,
valet parking, and 24-hour concierge. Owners enjoy direct Atlantic Ocean frontage on Collins Avenue.

Rental Policy: Minimum lease term is 6 months unless board approval is granted for shorter stays.
No daily or weekly transient rentals are permitted under the offering plan.

Pet Policy: Dogs and cats are allowed subject to a 35-pound weight limit and a one-pet maximum
per unit unless the board grants an exception.

Assessment Notes: Capital reserves are funded at 8% of annual operating budget per offering plan section 4.2.
