Continuum South Beach — HOA Rules (Fixture)

Amenities: Full-service spa, two oceanfront pools, private marina slips, tennis courts,
and a residents-only beach club with cabanas.

Pet Policy: Maximum two pets per unit; breed restrictions apply for dogs over 50 pounds.
All pets must be registered with the association within 30 days of move-in.

Rental Restrictions: Minimum rental period is 30 days. No Airbnb or short-term vacation rentals.
Owners must submit lease copies to the management office.

House Rules: Quiet hours 10pm–8am. Move-in/out only on weekdays with 72-hour notice.
